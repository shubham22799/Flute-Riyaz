Sargam notation used here

Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni = shuddha · prefix k = komal (kRe kGa kDha kNi) · prefix t = tivra (tMa).
Octave: ’Sa = mandra saptak · Sa = madhya saptak · Sa’ = taar saptak.
Western notes use sharps with A4 = 440 Hz by default (configurable).

Honesty notes

• Detected durations are estimates derived from audio events, not physiological breath measurements.
• Sargam mapping is equal-tempered relative to your confirmed Sa — a practical starting point, not a claim about raga intonation.
• LIVE MICROPHONE and SIMULATION are always visibly distinguished; mic failure never silently swaps in fake readings.
• All data stays in this browser (localStorage) — no account, no cloud.

Tip: serve this folder over http://localhost (e.g. python3 -m http.server) — browsers restrict microphone access on file:// pages.
